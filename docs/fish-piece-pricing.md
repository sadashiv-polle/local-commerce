# Fixed piece pricing for fish

Create a separate product using **Add product → By pieces**. Its stock unit is Nos.
Set the selling price per piece (for example, ₹150). A customer choosing quantity 3
pays ₹450 before configured delivery charges and taxes. No packed-weight confirmation
is required, and differing fish weights do not change the agreed price.

Receive stock in pieces and enter cost per piece. Receiving 50 pieces and fulfilling
3 leaves 47 pieces; pending orders reserve availability. Expiry and wastage also use
pieces. Allocate received fish between weight and piece products without double counting.

Daily price changes apply to new orders. Existing orders retain their native Sales Order
prices. Keep Kg products separate. Do not change an existing item's stock unit or convert
historical transactions just to switch selling mode. Archive unused older offers as needed.

Customers select individual pieces with the quantity control; this is not a separate
fixed-size packet inventory system.
